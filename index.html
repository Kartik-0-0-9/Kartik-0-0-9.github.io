Research

MS Thesis: Probabilistic Chronology Modeling for Paleoclimate Proxies
Advisor: Prof. Bedartha Goswami, IISER Pune · Jul 2026 – present

Age–depth models for proxy records (e.g. stalagmites) are usually built first and treated as fixed ground truth for everything downstream. That throws away real uncertainty in the radiometric dating itself. I'm building a framework that instead propagates that chronological uncertainty directly into a proxy-time Gaussian Process, so the age model and the climate signal are inferred jointly rather than in sequence.

  • Hybrid deep learning scaling layer to cut the O(N³) cost of spatio-temporal GP inference, so sparse, irregularly-dated proxy records can be mapped onto continuous global grids.
  • Builds on and extends prior GP-scaling work (Axen et al.) for the specific structure of chronological uncertainty.
CAPE-Net: long-range monsoon forecasting
Project Student, DST Centre of Excellence, IIT Delhi · Advisor: Dr. Saroj Kanta Mishra · May – Jul 2026

Decadal prediction of the Indian summer monsoon requires linking two very different kinds of data: large multi-model climate simulation ensembles and comparatively sparse ocean observations. CAPE-Net (Cross-Attention Predictive Ensemble Network) is a PyTorch architecture I built to align CMIP6 simulations with observed sea-surface temperature and ocean heat content through cross-attention, producing a 10-year-lead forecast.

  • Out-of-distribution detector (Mahalanobis distance with Ledoit–Wolf shrinkage) that flags ocean initial conditions the model hasn't seen the like of, rather than letting it forecast blindly.
  • Checked the model wasn't just pattern-matching: gradient-based saliency maps confirm the learned latent features track known marine teleconnections.
  • Wrote the spatial preprocessing pipeline that feeds the model: EOF/PCA decomposition followed by Geopandas regionalization across six subcontinental climate zones.
Gaussian Processes for Paleoclimatological Age Modeling
Advisor: Prof. Bedartha Goswami, IISER Pune · Aug 2024 – Jul 2025

The undergraduate project the MS thesis grew out of. Standard GP treatments of proxy dating assume the measured signal and the radiometric time are conditionally independent given depth. That's convenient, but not physically true. I formulated a joint GP over both quantities without that assumption, then extended it with monotonicity constraints (via virtual derivative observations) to enforce the one thing standard non-parametric GPs don't guarantee for free: that depth and time can't run backwards. Validated against synthetic benchmark datasets built for the purpose.

  • Code: github.com/Kartik-0-0-9/Monotonization-of-Gaussian-Processes
